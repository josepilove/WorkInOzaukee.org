There are four different zones here. Today I'm at the bar up front, and it's nice for
some solo work — plenty of outlets, and the chairs are reasonably comfortable
bar-height chairs. Taking a call here wouldn't work; it's too open and usually too
loud, though the music isn't too loud where I am today.

I appreciate that the furniture is all pretty substantial. There are a couple of
different areas that are good for meeting with up to six people.

Confirmed the hours at the counter: closed Mondays, 7–3 the rest of the week, 9–3 on
Sunday.
