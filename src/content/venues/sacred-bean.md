Runs out of the Cavelier Wine Bar space on East Jackson — same room, opposite end of
the clock. Sacred Bean has it mornings Thursday through Sunday; Cavelier opens at
three.

The coffee is top-notch. It's the best cortado in town.

Adam, the owner, is incredibly friendly and loves people coming in and working here as
part of their routine — expect to have your order memorized and to be greeted by name
each morning.

The music is pretty even all over, and the room is geared more toward a bar/social
atmosphere than a heads-down one. Outlets are only at the three big concrete pillars
in the space, so if you need power, that's where you sit.

The big windows can make it get pretty hot when it's sunny. There's a front and a back
patio, which are the quietest spots, but space out there is limited. Always plenty of
parking.
